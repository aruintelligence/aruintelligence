# ĀRU Public Research Standards

ĀRU Intelligence publishes experimental software and theoretical artifacts to make ideas inspectable. Public work should make clear what is implemented, what is simulated, what is hypothesized, and what remains untested.

## Evidence labels

Use the following language consistently:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Implemented** | The behavior exists in the repository and can be inspected or run. |
| **Simulated** | The behavior appears inside a model whose variables are defined by that model. |
| **Observed** | A result was measured under stated conditions and should include methods and data. |
| **Hypothesized** | A proposed explanation or relationship that requires testing. |
| **Metaphorical** | Symbolic language used to guide design or interpretation. |
| **Validated** | Independently replicated or supported by an appropriate validation process. |

Do not convert a simulated value into an empirical claim. Do not describe metaphorical state names as evidence of consciousness, agency, spirituality, or physical law.

## Minimum project documentation

A public research repository should identify:

1. The question it explores.
2. The maturity of the work.
3. A reproducible way to run it.
4. Dependencies and supported runtime.
5. Expected output.
6. Known limitations.
7. License and authorship.
8. A route for responsible disclosure.
9. Related publications or persistent identifiers, when applicable.

## Reproducibility

- Prefer deterministic examples with explicit seeds.
- Record versions of material dependencies.
- Separate sample output from measured results.
- State hardware, data, and environment assumptions.
- Preserve historical versions without presenting them as equally current.
- Use persistent citations for formal publications.

## Responsible communication

ĀRU Intelligence welcomes criticism that is specific, reproducible, and made in good faith. Claims should be proportional to evidence. Corrections should be recorded transparently, and uncertainty should remain visible.

## Safety and human agency

Public prototypes must not be presented as substitutes for medical, psychological, legal, financial, or spiritual authority. Systems intended to influence decisions should expose their constraints and preserve accountable human choice.

## Attribution

Credit should reflect actual contribution. Tool assistance, including AI assistance, may be disclosed where material. Trademark claims and open-source licenses are separate: a software license governs code use, while marks identify projects and organizations.
