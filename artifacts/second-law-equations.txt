Proposed relation: ∇ · R(x,t) = α · ∇²μ(x,t).
R(x,t): memory reaction field (the record's name for a structure-forming vector field).
μ(x,t): normalized memory density (the record states bits/m³).
α: structural resonance coefficient (the record leaves its units medium-dependent).

This text reproduces the PDF's formal expression, not evidence that R, μ, or α are measured physical quantities. The equation alone does not specify boundary conditions or a unique R.
