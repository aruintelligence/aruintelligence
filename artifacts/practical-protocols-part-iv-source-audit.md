## The practical contribution

Part IV supplies fourteen chapters, **41–54**, with a common practical orientation. Chapter 41 names pre-state measurement, intervention, observation and post-state measurement, then asks the reader to define a question, change, measure and success criterion. That scaffold can support a more rigorous future protocol if each term is operationalized. The rest provide short exercises on breath, memory, relationship reciprocity, coherence, emotional charge, attention, identity and diagramming.

The PDF calls several practices “experiments” and mentions measurement, but reports **no completed trials, raw data, participant counts, instrument calibration, comparisons or statistical analyses**. Exp. 017 is a tractable place to specify a test rather than assume that a breathing correlation occurred. The source-audited value here is the authored protocol inventory and the Chapter 41 structure.
