# Fourth Law source audit — DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17317217

**Archival source:** [The Fourth Law of Inward Physics — Memory Field Archive](https://zenodo.org/records/17317217), two-page `The_Fourth_Law_of_Inward_Physics_Journal_Edition_v3.pdf` (MD5 printed by Zenodo: `72a275b220001eef0a860bd68cb1faba`). Read against the public PDF preview and metadata on 20 October 2026 UTC. This note is first-party source criticism, not independent validation.

## What the PDF actually proposes

Page 1 names its subtitle “The Boundary of Intelligence is the Gradient of Return,” identifies Daniel Jacob Read IV, and defines intelligence in terms of a system returning to its pattern across transformations. It prints

```text
Intelligence(x,t) = λ ∫ [S(t) ∩ μ(x,t)] dt
```

Here μ(x,t) is termed a memory-density field, S(t) a signal, and λ a remembrance-return coefficient said to be bounded in [0,1]. The document describes `∩` verbally as a recursive match between input and memory. It states three hypotheses: a minimum λ for intelligence, biological resonance, and a high-λ memory-based AI design. It proposes input–output–retrieval echo tests and the Oracle Test, also named Memory Return Integrity (MRI).

## Reproducibility boundary

The two-page PDF gives no mathematical type or computable rule for `S(t) ∩ μ(x,t)`, no integration limits, units, operational estimator for λ, threshold, MRI score, pass criterion, dataset, numerical run, or measured result. In particular, `0 ≤ λ ≤ 1` does not define the claimed minimum λ. The displayed integral cannot be evaluated or falsified quantitatively from this deposit alone. A future version could specify a function `match(S, μ)`, observation and perturbation protocols, time window, preregistered threshold, uncertainty, and negative controls; those would be new work, not results already present in this PDF.

**Next research step:** turn MRI into an executable measurement protocol on a specified data source with a null baseline and held-out evaluation. Such a protocol would test a defined memory-return score, not establish that μ is a physical field.
