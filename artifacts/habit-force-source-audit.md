The piece offers a clear public-facing conceptual distinction: a repeated phrase can participate in a behavior loop without itself guaranteeing an outcome. It argues that habits and environmental reinforcement can be interrupted. The deposited PDF has **no probability equation, data, participant sample, intervention trial, effect-size estimate or citations**. “Habit force” is not yet an operational measure, and the diagram does not establish universal claims such as every loop being interruptible in every context. Its value as an essay and a hypothesis does not require presenting those propositions as measured behavioral results.

For a later empirical edition, define exposure to repeated speech separately from attention and behavior; specify an outcome and observation window, prospective measures for each link, a control or alternative explanation, and an interruption protocol with a null result criterion. Distinguish a normative agency message from an estimated causal effect. The original five-page archive need not be silently replaced to do that work.

## Public metadata and author choices

The public description calls this a structural analysis and says it “demonstrates” and “shows” probabilistic outcomes without reporting a model or study. A source-grounded description should instead call it a conceptual essay proposing a speech–attention–behavior–environment pathway and a critique of inevitability claims. The PDF subtitle says **“Structural Clarification”** where the Zenodo title says **“Structural Analysis”**; retain the archived PDF, then let the record owner decide whether to align the public subtitle to the deposited title or explicitly note the variant. The page shows creator `Read, Daniel Jacob` with matching ORCID `0009-0000-6133-1872`, rendered `Version Version: 1.0`, a single comma-packed subject, and restrictive copyright without a reuse license. The author suffix is supported by the PDF's copyright line. Split a small set of source-backed subjects, clean the version field, and preserve rights. [Issue #35](https://github.com/aruintelligence/aruintelligence/issues/35) tracks these choices. No Zenodo metadata was edited in this signed-out pass.
