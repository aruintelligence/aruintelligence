The file identifies itself as based on the First Law. The governing equation has a bounded conditional implication worth making explicit. In a spatially homogeneous setting with `D` irrelevant, constant `S=S₀` and `λ>0`, it yields

```text
μ(t) = μ₀ + S₀/λ + [μ(0) − μ₀ − S₀/λ]e^(−λt).
```

Thus the stipulated field relaxes toward `μ₀+S₀/λ`. That result alone has no matter-condensation threshold, consciousness coupling law or observed manifestation endpoint. If the inverse-μ time expression is used, μ also needs a nonzero positive domain and κ_t units. A later technical version could define the source S independently of its predicted outcome, state dimensions and boundary data, and propose a comparison with ordinary diffusion/relaxation and attention-independent controls. The interpretive writing remains a distinct creative contribution.
