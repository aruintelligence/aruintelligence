The contribution is a **single inspectable research program**: field postulates, a minimal dynamical ansatz, three candidate experiments and a list of confounds. Its mathematical statements are conditional on an as-yet-unmeasured μ. The mass-perturbation formula follows by linearity when κ_M and Ω are fixed; it does not independently measure μ or show that attention changes mass. The inverse clock relation requires μ>0 as postulated and a calibrated κ_t to predict a rate. The awareness derivative needs an operational awareness parameter and a source coupling `S(a)` before it defines an experimental intervention.

The paper responsibly proposes randomized/sham blocks, environmental shielding, preregistration, raw data/code release, and replication. The `10⁻⁹–10⁻⁷` relative mass range is an **order-of-magnitude target** in the PDF, not a result or a derivation from S and κ_M. A testable next step is to fix the device/source mechanism, predicted sign and effect size with uncertainty, an instrument-specific null bound, matched non-attention controls, and a preregistered analysis. Its later [Methods & Constants Addendum](./first-law-addendum-source-audit.md) chooses `μ=1−Φ/c²` to recover familiar gravity and clock limits by construction; the [2026 field-theoretic First Law audit](./first-law-formal-source-audit.md) treats a distinct, later formulation. These deposits should retain separate DOI identities.
