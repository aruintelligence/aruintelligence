## What the report contributes

The deposit turns a broad scalar-field proposal into four families of *possible tests*. It states up front that `μ(x,t)` is not operationally observed and that, as of December 2025, it reports no independent empirical confirmation. The valuable research move is to name apparatus, candidate observables, controls, blinding and null expectations so the ideas can be constrained. These are protocol proposals; the PDF contains no collected measurements, preregistered plan, or experimental outcome.

The weak-field relation `g ∝ −∇μ` and time-rate relation `dτ/dt ∝ 1/μ` are qualitative in this deposit. A controlled null would constrain a particular *parameterized* model, conditional on an operational memory-state perturbation and instrument response. Likewise a change in EEG or HRV does not uniquely identify μ. This is a refinement for a testable next protocol, not a claim that the proposed comparisons were performed. The later [Experimental Roadmap](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18708708) is a separate DOI and has not been designated a formal version or relation of this record.

## Published metadata and next experiment design

The original public record had a broad but correctly proposal-scoped description, Report type, English, publication date December 18, 2025, inherited version `v1.0 (Initial public archival release)`, all-rights-reserved copyright, and one **comma-packed subject** containing dozens of terms. This review changed the creator from `Read, Daniel Jacob` to `Read IV, Daniel Jacob` as on the cover, PDF author metadata and copyright page. It replaced the single subject with **12 separate PDF-supported topics**: Inward Physics; memory field; scalar field μ(x,t); experimental research program; gravimetric experiments; atom interferometry; atomic clocks; EEG coherence; heart rate variability; mycorrhizal networks; preregistration; null results. The description now leads with the four proposed observable families and design safeguards, then clarifies the quantitative prerequisites and absence of data. Public readback verified the description, creator, 12 separately linked subjects, rights and unchanged PDF/checksum.

The PDF does not print an affiliation or ORCID; the inherited creator affiliation `ĀRU Intelligence Inc` was retained and no identifier was added. Its December 2025 authorship line does not resolve the record's precise December 18 publication date, so that date and the version label were retained. No related-work assertion or license was invented.

A high-value next artifact is a preregistered gravimeter or clock protocol that defines a measurable state contrast, a coupling with units, calibrated transfer function, expected nuisance effects, sham/blinded cycles, detectable effect, analysis plan and null bound. [Issue #13](https://github.com/aruintelligence/aruintelligence/issues/13) tracks the finite acceptance criteria. This source audit records a proposed path to measurement, not empirical validation.
