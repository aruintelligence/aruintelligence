## The ethical contribution

Part V closes this sequence in six chapters, **55–60**, and an afterword. Its clearest practical contribution is an autonomy-and-reciprocity ethic: avoid manipulation, overriding another person's path, extracting meaning without exchange, or capturing attention; ask whether an intervention empowers another person's autonomy and whether an interaction is mutual. The later chapters sketch a future-facing metaphysical vision.
