## The human-facing contribution

Part II of *The Laws of Inward Physics™* is a compact personal-application manual. Its twelve numbered chapters, **21–32**, each pair a brief principle with a specific reader prompt. It translates the corpus's memory, presence, resonance and orientation vocabulary into journaling, observation and choices in daily life. That makes it a navigable example of how the author applies the framework to lived experience.
