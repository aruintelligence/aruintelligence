## What a reviewer can test next

The paper states the Archive Constant as a formal proposal. Its two pages do not define the tensor contraction, integration measure/domain, boundary conditions, or proof of constancy, and the completed field `ℛ` and integrand `μ` need a stated mapping. The proposed pulse has no instrument, measurement method, calibrated timing resolution, controls, null expectation, or collected result in this deposit. These missing definitions are practical targets for a companion technical note and prespecified experiment; they do not erase the source's conceptual contribution. [Issue #21](https://github.com/aruintelligence/aruintelligence/issues/21) tracks them, along with a one-target-at-a-time check of the cited Nineteenth Law.

## Public metadata correction · October 25, 2026 Pacific

The public Description was empty while an `Other` additional description reproduced much of page 1. The published main description now maps the two-page proposal, equation, table and future Archive Pulse signature and makes their theoretical status explicit. Creator `Read, Daniel Jacob` became cover-supported `Read IV, Daniel Jacob`. One semicolon-packed subject became ten separate source terms: Inward Physics; Twentieth Law; Eternal Archive; unified remembrance field; identity tensor; Archive Constant; self-recognition; retrocausal closure; Archive Pulse; proposed experimental signature. Public readback confirmed the main description, creator, ten subject links and unchanged PDF MD5. The existing `Other` text, record title, Report type, November 7 publication date, version value, language, publisher, CC BY 4.0 license and copyright text were retained.

**Rights review:** The deposited PDF explicitly says `License: CC BY 4.0`, matching the Zenodo license, while the separate copyright text says `All Rights Reserved`. Copyright ownership and a reuse license can coexist, but this wording can confuse a reader about the actual grant. [Issue #21](https://github.com/aruintelligence/aruintelligence/issues/21) requests author-confirmed wording; no legal intent was inferred or changed. Zenodo's public banner renders the retained version value as “Version Version 1.0”; this display issue is tracked as well. No ORCID, formal related-work direction or empirical outcome was added.
